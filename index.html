THE IPM METHOD / HOW WE WORK

Diagnose before we treat

Most companies spray first and ask questions never. Our process starts with the lawn, not the truck.

STEP — 01

Inspect

Walk the property. Turf condition, ornamentals, irrigation, shade, drainage — the full picture.

STEP — 02

Identify

Pinpoint the actual pest or disease — chinch bugs, grubs, fungus, weeds — not a guess.

STEP — 03

Treat

Apply the right product, at the right rate, only where it's needed. Targeted, not broadcast.

STEP — 04

Monitor

Follow-up checks to confirm it worked — and adjust the plan before problems return.
